How to Play

Moving

Touch and drag anywhere to move. Your weapons fire automatically. (On a keyboard: WASD or arrow keys.)

Missions

Pick any county for your first mission. After that you can only attack counties that border one you have liberated. Each new county has one more wave than the last, and zombies grow tougher the further you push. Survive every wave, then slay the horde boss to liberate the county.

Counter-attacks

The horde fights back. A liberated county bordering overrun land may come under attack (it flashes red on the map). Defend it by winning a mission there within 3 missions, or it falls back to the zombies.

Survivors and bosses

Buy new survivors with coins: each starts with a different weapon and perks. Every region has its own horde boss, from Nessie in the Highlands to the Stonehenge Druid. Watch for red warning circles: get out before the slam lands!

Levelling up in a mission

Zombies drop XP gems. Each level lets you pick a new weapon, a passive item, or an upgrade. You can carry 6 weapons and 6 passives. Elite zombies drop treasure chests.

Evolutions

Max out a weapon while holding its partner passive to evolve it. Max out the Ice, Fire and Electric Swords together to forge the legendary Sword of Light. See the Armoury for every recipe.

Coins and account level

Coins carry over between missions (even if you fall) and buy permanent stat upgrades. XP you collect also raises your account level, which unlocks new weapons and passives that can then turn up in missions.
